🙊 TABOO LAB
Describe the word — without saying the forbidden words!

How to play

  1. One player per team is the describer. Only they look at the screen.
  2. They describe the target word so their team can guess it — but they can't say the word itself or any of the forbidden words under it.
  3. No spelling, no "rhymes with," no hand gestures — only talking!
  4. Team guesses it before the timer? +1 point, next word! Stuck or rule broken? Skip it.
Classroom setup: the describer stands by the laptop or holds a tablet, facing their team, so only they see the word. The class screen can stay on the big timer and scores. Rotate the describer each round so everyone gets a turn to talk.
