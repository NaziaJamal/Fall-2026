Class 1 · Trolley5
Engage

Five vs. one — both times. So what changed?

Round 1 · The switch

Most people pull it.

Utilitarian: five lives > one life. (Bentham)

Round 2 · The footbridge

Most people refuse.

Kant: you'd be using a person merely as a means.

The numbers didn't change. Our relationship to the people did.

Class 1 · Study11
Study

Stakeholder ≠ stockholder

Stakeholder

anyone with an interest in how a business makes its profit

Stockholder / shareholder

someone who owns shares

Internal: board, CEO, employees
External: customers, suppliers, governments

One person can wear several hats: an employee who is also a customer and a shareholder. (3.1)

The ethics thermometer 8 min

❄ Below the law
Legal minimum
Beyond the law
Ethical maximum 🔥

Ethical minimum = the least a company can do while still obeying the law.
Ethical maximum = the strongest ethical action possible. (3.1)

Normative = stakeholders matter in themselves.
Instrumental = treat them well because it pays. (3.2)

Place the six case cards. Then decide each motive: normative or instrumental?

Lumen Data wants to build an AI data center on farmland outside Harbor Bay (pop. 18,000), a coastal town with dry summers.

Class 2 · Town hall32
Round 1 · Opening statements

45 seconds each 12 min

The Chair calls each group. The bell means stop.

The Gazette may ask one question after any statement.

Try: "With respect, that doesn't address…" · "Could you clarify what you mean by…?" · "No one here is speaking for…"
Class 2 · Town hall33
Round 2

The lobby 10 min

  • Move around. Make deals. "We'll support you if…"
  • Every deal is written on a slip and signed by both groups.
  • The Council listens — but can't make deals.
Try: "Would you be willing to…?" · "What if we agreed to…?" · "We're prepared to compromise on ___, but not on…" · "That's non-negotiable for us."
Class 2 · Town hall34
Rounds 3 & 4

Final offers → the Council decides 4 + 5 min

  1. Final offers: 20 seconds per group. Announce your deals.
  2. Fishbowl: the Council deliberates in the middle. Everyone else is silent. The Council must name:
    • the key stakeholders
    • the test it used
    • one voiceless stakeholder it considered
  3. Vote. Announce the decision and conditions. Then: the Gazette's headline.
Class 2 · Town hall35
Debrief

Step out of role. Whose claim won — and why?

  • Read your secrets aloud. Which changed the outcome? Which never came out?
  • Were the key stakeholders the ones you put top right this morning?
  • Which test did the Council use? Was that ethical — or just practical?
  • Who spoke for the river and for future residents? Was it enough?
  • Which Week 2 framework does the decision reflect: utilitarian, Kantian, Rawlsian?
