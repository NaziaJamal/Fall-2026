Harbor Bay: a data center decision (fictional case)

Lumen Data, a technology company, wants to build a large AI data center on farmland outside Harbor Bay (population 18,000), a coastal town with dry summers.

  • A $1.2 billion investment; about 1,500 construction jobs for two years, then 120 permanent jobs.
  • In return: a 10-year property-tax break. After that, about $9 million a year in local taxes.
  • The cooling system would use up to 2 million liters of water a day from the town's supply.
  • The center would use as much electricity as a small city. The utility says residents' electricity rates may rise.
  • Lumen offers a $500,000-a-year community fund.

The Town Council will vote: approve · approve with conditions · delay for an independent study · reject.

Task: Who are the stakeholders? Place them on the power–interest matrix. Who is voiceless? Who are the key stakeholders?
