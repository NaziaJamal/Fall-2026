Class 1 · Activate · Chiquita15
What actually happened

Chiquita Brands International

  • Paid "protection" money to armed groups in Colombia, 1997–2004.
  • Said it was extorted. Paid $25 million in U.S. fines (Foreign Corrupt Practices Act).
  • 2007: Colombian families sued. 2015: U.S. Supreme Court declined the case. (Textbook, 2.3)
  • 2024: a U.S. federal jury found Chiquita liable for financing the AUC paramilitary group — about $38 million to 16 victims' families. Chiquita appealed. (EarthRights; Bloomberg Law)

Class 2 · Timeline29
Answers

2,500 years in ten ideas

551–479 BCEConfuciusBecome a junzi; keep proper order (li)
508 BCECleisthenesOpen democracy in Athens
384–322 BCEAristotleVirtue is the mean; aim at flourishing
13th c.AquinasExpands Aristotle; the parts of prudence
1588–1679HobbesSelfish people give power to a sovereign
1724–1804KantAct on a rule you could will as universal law
1748–1832BenthamGreatest happiness for the greatest number
1806–1873MillLimit freedom only to prevent harm to others
1921–2002RawlsChoose the rules behind a veil of ignorance
1938–2002NozickThe state may not take legally obtained property

What changes? character & harmony → consequences → duty → fair systems
