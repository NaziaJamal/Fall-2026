Exercise 2Part One Activation — Police Encounters

Guiding question: "Have you ever had to navigate a legal situation here? What did you learn?"

A. Conversation — talk with a partner

Have you ever had to navigate a legal situation here (or do you know someone who has)? What did you learn from the experience? Discuss with a partner, then write one thing you learned from your partner's story.

B. Share Your Opinion — answer on your own

What is the one tip from today about police encounters that you think is most important to remember? Be ready to share your answer with the class.

C. Writing — write 3–5 sentences

Describe what you would say if a police officer stopped you and asked you questions.

Exercise 3Part Two Activation — Tenant Rights

Guiding question: "How is the landlord-tenant relationship here different from your home country?"

A. Conversation — talk with a partner

How is the landlord-tenant relationship here different from your home country? Discuss with a partner, then write one difference you talked about.

B. Share Your Opinion — answer on your own

Which tenant right from today (heat, security deposit, or lease terms) do you think is the most important? Why?

C. Writing — write a short paragraph

Compare renting a home in the U.S. to renting a home in your home country.

Exercise 4Part Three Activation — Government Offices

Guiding question: "What is your experience of dealing with government offices in America vs. at home?"

A. Conversation — talk with a partner

What is your experience of dealing with government offices in America vs. at home? Discuss with a partner, then write one similarity or difference you talked about.

B. Share Your Opinion — answer on your own

Which office (City Hall, RMV, Social Security Administration, or USCIS) do you think is the most confusing for newcomers? Why?

C. Writing — write step-by-step instructions

Describe a task you completed at a government office (for example, getting an ID or applying for benefits). Write each step on a new line.
