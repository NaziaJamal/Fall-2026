How to play
  1. Stand up and walk around the room. The timer is already running!
  2. Tap a square. Ask a classmate the question in English.
  3. If they say yes, ask the follow-up question. Listen, then type their name.
  4. Use a different classmate for each square.
  5. Complete a full line (→ ↓ ✕) and say “BINGO!”

How to make the question

Card says
Has experienced…
You ask
Have you ever experienced…?
Answer: Yes, I have. / No, I haven't.
Card says
Thinks / Believes / Feels…
You ask
Do you think / believe / feel…?
Answer: Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
If YES → ask a follow-up: “Why?” · “What happened?” · “Can you give me an example?”
If NO → “Thanks anyway!” Then find someone else.
