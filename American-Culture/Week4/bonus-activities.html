School Talk: Bonus Round!

Three extra activities about school life — with a partner, in a group, and with the whole class.

1

Two Truths and a Lie: School Days

Pairs10–12 min
  1. Write three sentences about your own school days. Two are true. One is a lie!
  2. Mark which one is the lie, then click Hide my lie before you show your partner.
  3. Read your sentences to your partner. Your partner asks up to 3 questions, then guesses the lie.
  4. Switch roles. Play two rounds — keep score below!

Need ideas? Start with:

"When I was in school, I…""My favorite subject was…""I once got in trouble for…""My school had…""Every day, I…"

2

Rank It! What Makes a Great School?

Groups of 3–415 min
  1. Read the 8 school features below.
  2. As a group, put them in order from most important (1) to least important (8). Use the arrows to move them.
  3. You must all agree — discuss, disagree politely, and decide!
  4. Compare your top 3 and your number 8 with another group. What's different?
    Useful language for agreeing & disagreeing "I think ___ should be number one because…" · "I see your point, but…" · "I agree with you." · "What about ___?" · "Can we agree on…?" · "OK, let's compromise."
    3

    The Opinion Line

    Whole Class15 min · Move!
    1. For each statement, choose your opinion from 1 to 5 and write one reason.
    2. Your teacher reads a statement. Stand on the line in the room where your opinion is:
    ← 1 Strongly disagree3 Not sureStrongly agree 5 →
    1. Talk to the people next to you: are your reasons the same?
    2. The teacher asks someone from each end to explain. Listen — you can move if you change your mind!
    Useful language "I strongly agree / disagree because…" · "In my country, …" · "That's a good point." · "I'm not sure, but…" · "I changed my mind because…"
