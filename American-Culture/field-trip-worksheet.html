Part 2  ·  15 points

My Reflection

Write 6–8 sentences. Answer these questions in your paragraph:

  1. Which place was the most interesting for you? Why?
  2. What is one thing you learned today that surprised you?
  3. How does one of these places connect to something we studied in class (the Revolution, government, schools, or civil rights)?
  4. Is there a similar place or story in your home country? Compare them.

Useful phrases: "The most interesting place was… because…" · "I was surprised to learn that…" · "This reminds me of… in my country." · "In Week __, we learned that…"
