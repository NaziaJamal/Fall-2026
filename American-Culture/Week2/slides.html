How did the USA become 50 states?

It started with just 13 colonies on the east coast…

🤔 Think about it: How does a country go from 13 to 50 states?

The Story Begins
1607

The First English Colony: Virginia

  • English settlers land at Jamestown, Virginia
  • It is the first permanent English settlement in North America
  • Life is very hard — disease, hunger, and conflict with Native Americans
  • But they survive. And more colonies follow.
FUN FACT"Virginia" was named after Queen Elizabeth I — the "Virgin Queen" of England
💬 "Imagine crossing the Atlantic Ocean in a small wooden ship. Why would someone do that?"
170 Years Later…
1776

Independence! The 13 Colonies Break Free

  • 13 colonies declare independence from Britain
  • The Declaration of Independence is signed on July 4th
  • A long war follows — the American Revolution (1775–1783)
  • The 13 colonies become the first 13 states of the USA
The Original 13 States 1. Virginia

🎆 July 4th is still celebrated today as Independence Day!
The Rulebook
1787

The U.S. Constitution — The Rulebook for the Country

WHAT IS IT?A document that sets the rules for how the government works
SIGNED WHERE?Philadelphia, Pennsylvania — September 1787
STILL USED?YES! It is the oldest written national constitution still in use
  • It creates three branches of government: Congress, the President, and the Courts
  • It protects the rights of every American citizen
  • It can be changed (called an "amendment") — but it is very difficult
💬 "What do you think is the most important rule a country needs?"
The Country Grows
1803

The Louisiana Purchase: The USA Doubles in Size

  • The U.S. buys a huge territory from France (Napoleon needs money!)
  • The purchase covers land from the Mississippi River to the Rocky Mountains
  • Price paid: about $15 million — roughly 3 cents per acre
  • This opens the door for westward expansion across North America
HOW BIG?About 828,000 square miles — 23% of the USA today
FROM WHOM?Napoleon Bonaparte, Emperor of France. He needed cash for war.
💡 States created from this purchase include: Louisiana, Missouri, Arkansas, Iowa, and more!
1840s–1850s

Go West! Texas, California & the Southwest Join

1845
Texas joins the USA
It was its own independent country before — the Republic of Texas!
1846
Oregon Territory agreed with Britain
Peaceful negotiation — the border is set at the 49th parallel
1848
California & the Southwest after the war with Mexico
Gold discovered in California in 1848 — the Gold Rush begins!
⭐ GOLD RUSH"Go West, young man!"

About 300,000 people flooded into California after 1848
💬 "Why do you think so many people were willing to travel thousands of miles to find gold?"
A Surprising Purchase
1867

Alaska: Bought from Russia for $7.2 Million

  • Russia needs money after the Crimean War — they offer Alaska to the USA
  • U.S. Secretary of State William Seward agrees to buy it
  • Many Americans laugh and call it "Seward's Folly" (a foolish mistake)
  • But Alaska has oil, gold, fish, and timber — worth trillions today!
  • It becomes the 49th state in 1959
HOW BIG?Largest U.S. state — bigger than Texas, California & Montana combined!
PRICE PER ACREAbout 2 cents per acre. The best deal in U.S. history?
The Last Piece
1959

Hawaii: The 50th and Final State

ONLY ISLAND STATEMade up of 8 major islands in the Pacific Ocean
LAST TO JOINAugust 21, 1959 — 183 years after the 13 colonies declared independence
PEARL HARBORDecember 7, 1941 — the attack that brought the U.S. into WWII
BIRTHPLACEPresident Barack Obama was born in Honolulu, Hawaii

Useful Language: Describing States

___ is known for its ___ and ___.e.g. New York is known for its skyline and diversity.
___ is located in the ___ part of the USA.e.g. Florida is located in the southeastern United States.
Compared to ___, ___ is much ___.e.g. Compared to Delaware, Texas is much larger.
One surprising fact about ___ is that ___.e.g. One surprising fact about Alaska is that it was bought for just $7.2 million.
___ joined the USA in ___, when ___.e.g. Hawaii joined the USA in 1959, when it became the 50th state.
