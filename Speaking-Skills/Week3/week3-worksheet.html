D4 · Fix the ramble

Semi-controlledThen compare

This speaker is rambling. Rewrite it with at least four signposts so the audience can follow. You can change the order.

"Libraries are great. They have free classes. When I moved here I didn't know anyone. You can borrow movies. I made friends in the conversation group. It's free. My English got better. Also there's free Wi-Fi. I think everyone should go."
Model: "Today I want to talk about a place most people forget: the public library. I'll share three reasons it changed my life here. First, it's free: free classes, free movies, even free Wi-Fi. Second, it helped my English. When I moved here, I didn't know anyone, so I joined the conversation group. That brings me to the third reason: I made real friends there. So here's my question to you: when will you visit your library?"
