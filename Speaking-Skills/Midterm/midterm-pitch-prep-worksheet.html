ACollect what you already have

Individual · 10 min · Look back at your Weeks 2–5 worksheets.

Week 2 · My idea

What is your talk about, in a few words?

Week 3 · My one listener

A real person: curious, smart, not an expert.

Week 4 · My story

Your story in 2–3 sentences: when, where, what happened.

Week 5 · My reasons

Why should the audience care? List 2–3 reasons or pieces of evidence.

BSharpen your throughline

Individual → Pairs · 10 min

B3 · The repeat test

Say your throughline once to a partner. Don't show it. Your partner repeats it in their own words.

B2+ challenge: write a second version as a cleft sentence: "What I've learned is that…" or "The thing nobody tells you about ___ is…"

CHook lab: three openings, one winner

Individual → Pairs · 15 min · Your hook has about 15–20 seconds.

1 · A question

Have you ever…? / What would you do if…?

2 · A surprising fact

Here's something surprising: … / One in three…

3 · The first line of a story

Three years ago, I… / On my first day…

C4 · Test them

Read all three to your partner. Your partner chooses the one that makes them most curious and says why.

Number ___ made me want to hear more because…

Number ___ felt a little like an ask because…

D3 · The connection check

EWrite your ending

Individual · 5 min · About 15–20 seconds. Bring the audience back to your throughline.

Model: "So, asking for help isn't a weakness; it's a skill we can practice. Here's my question to you: who will you ask for help this week?"

FLanguage check

Individual → check · 10 min · Narrative tenses, signposts and clefts you'll use in your pitch.

F1 · Complete the pitch sentences

Use the word in brackets in the correct form, or write the missing word.

  1. Three years ago, I (move) to Boston.
  2. When I arrived, I (never / see) snow before.
  3. While I (walk) to class, I met my best friend.
  4. I want you to understand is that small habits matter.
  5. I'll share two things. , why we don't ask for help.
  6. For , when I started asking questions, my grades went up.
  7. That me to my second point.
  8. So, to up, asking for help is a skill.

F2 · Your own sentences

Write two sentences from your pitch: one in the past (from your story) and one signpost or cleft. Ask your partner or teacher to check them.

GMake your note cards

Individual · 10 min · Key words, not sentences. If you can read it aloud, it's too long.

B2: four cards, about 10 words or fewer on each.
B2+: minimal notes. Try one card only (the "B2+ one card" box) with about 15 words for the whole pitch.

Card 1 · Hook + throughline

Card 2 · Point 1 + support

Card 3 · Point 2 (+ 3) + support

Card 4 · Ending

B2+ one card · the whole pitch

Example card: First → we don't ask · look weak · library, 2 hours alone

IPractice pitch + partner feedback

Pairs · 15 min · Give your full pitch to a partner. Swap. Then fill this in with what your partner said about you.

One strength

Your throughline was clear when… / Your story made me care because…

One suggestion

One thing that would make it stronger is… / I got a little lost when…

B2+ challenge: no frames. Give one piece of delivery feedback and one piece of content feedback, and explain why each change would help the audience.

Final check: I'm ready when…
