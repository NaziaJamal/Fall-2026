The First Five Seconds

Before a speaker says a word, the audience has already decided:

Friend or foe?Likable or not?Confident or tentative?

Let's test it. Three mingle rounds. Greet two people each round. Then rate how much you trusted them.

Round 1 of 3

Eyes on your phone.

Walk around. Greet two people while you look at your phone or the floor.

Say"Hi! I have a story for you."
