Teacher-led
TenseJob in a storyExample

D4 · Human Timeline

GroupsMovement

Each person holds one event card. Round 1: line up in time order and tell the story. Round 2: the person with the ★ card steps forward. Line up in story order: start with the ★ moment, then go back in time. Change the tenses as you speak.

D5 · Tense upgrade: your story

FreerThen say it

Write the first moments of a true story of yours. Then say the four sentences to a partner, pausing after each time phrase.

1 · Time phrase + setting (Two years ago, … / The year I turned …, …)
2 · Past continuous: the scene (I was … -ing when …)
3 · Past perfect: background (I had never … / I had already …)
4 · Past simple: the moment everything changed (Then … / Suddenly …)
