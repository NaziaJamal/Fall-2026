Step 3 · Worksheet B3

Story Rescue

Groups · 7 min

Your group gets a badly told story. Rescue it!

  • Don't give away the point at the start
  • Cut three details we don't need
  • Add one feeling and one thing we can see or hear
  • Find a key line (something someone said)
  • End: funny, moving, or revealing

Plan · 3 min

Tell it in 45 seconds.

The class guesses: what did you cut?

Step 5 · Language Focus · Worksheet D1–D3

Your story toolkit

Study → Activate · 35 min
Past perfectthe back story
I had never been outside Nigeria.
Past continuousthe scene
I was waiting for the bus when…
Past simplethe main events
One Saturday, we went to his village.
would / used topast habits
My mother would say, "Finish your food!"

Time phrases = story signposts

Two years ago, … · The year I turned eight, … · Then one Saturday, … · Fifteen minutes later, … · Years later, … · Since then, …

Say it like a storyteller

  • Pause after a time phrase
  • Contractions: I'd never…
  • Slow down for the key line

Step 5 · Worksheet D4–D5

Human Timeline

Movement · 8 min

Round 1 · Time order

  • Read your card. Don't show it.
  • Find your group (A, B, C, D).
  • Line up in the order things happened.
  • Tell the story: one sentence each, past simple.

Round 2 · Story order

  • The ★ person steps to the front.
  • Start at the exciting moment, then go back in time.
  • Change your tense: had…, was …ing
  • Add a time phrase.
★ "I reached the desk, and my passport wasn't in my pocket. I had put it there the night before…"

Step 6 · Worksheet E3

Campfire Circles

Groups of 4 · 17 min

Lights down. Circle up. One "fire" in the middle.

Score 1–3 fingersAsk yourself
ConnectionDid they look at me and pause before the first word?
CharacterDid I care about the person in the story?
TensionDid I want to know what happened next?
DetailCould I see it? Not too little, not too much?
Resolution + pointWas the ending satisfying? Did it mean something?

Choose your Campfire Champion → tells the whole class. Class guesses the point.
