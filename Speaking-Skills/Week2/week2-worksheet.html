Put the spotlight on your idea

D1 · Notice

Pairs

b) What really matters is the first follower.

Now look at these sentences from today's talk and reading. Underline (or write) the part that gets the spotlight.

D2 · The patterns

Reference
PatternFormExample
What-cleftWhat + clause + is/was + FOCUSWhat I want you to remember is this.
It-cleftIt + is/was + FOCUS + that/who…It's the first follower who changes everything.
The thing / reasonThe (only) thing / reason / problem + clause + isThe reason this matters is that anyone can do it.
All-cleftAll + clause + isAll I'm asking is one minute a day.

Say it: pause before the focus, and stress it. "What really matters | is the FIRST follower."
