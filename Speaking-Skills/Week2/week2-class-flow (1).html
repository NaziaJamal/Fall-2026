Step 3 · Guided Reading · The big ideas

What Anderson wants you to understand

Study

1 · A talk is a gift

  • Sophie's talk was fun, and it changed how he sees laughter forever
  • Your mission: take something that matters to you and rebuild it in the listener's mind
ExampleSophie's idea: laughter turns social stress into a pleasant connection between people.

2 · An idea can be simple

  • Not just inventions or science
  • A how-to · a story with a lesson · an image · a hope · a reminder
  • An idea is anything that can change how people see the world
Example (a how-to)"Three questions to ask before you buy anything."

3 · "But I have nothing to say!"

  • Maybe it's just low confidence: ask people who know you
  • Your experience of life is unique, and a simple story can make a great talk
Ask yourselfWhat are you most proud of? When did someone last say, "That's really interesting!"?

Step 5 · Language Focus · Worksheet D1–D3

Cleft sentences: put the spotlight on it

Study · 35 min

NORMAL

The first follower matters.

CLEFT

What really matters is the first follower.

PatternFormExample
What-cleftWhat + clause + is + FOCUSWhat I want you to remember is this.
It-cleftIt's + FOCUS + that / who…It's the first follower who changes everything.
The reason…The reason / thing + clause + isThe reason this matters is that anyone can do it.
All-cleftAll + clause + isAll I'm asking is one minute a day.

Step 5 · Language Focus · Worksheet D4–D5

Why speakers use them, and how to say them

Study → Activate

Why?

  • The focus comes at the end, so the audience waits for it
  • It sounds clear and confident
  • It's easy to contrast: "It isn't X that matters; it's Y."
From the talk"It was really the first follower that transformed the lone nut into a leader."

Watch out

  • ✗ What I want it is… → ✓ What I want is…
  • ✗ It's my mother taught me… → ✓ It's my mother who taught me…
  • ✗ What mattered is… → ✓ What mattered was…

Say it like a speaker: pause | then stress

What really matters|is the FIRST follower.

Your turn: write your idea three ways (What… / It's… that… / The reason…). Your partner picks the strongest.
