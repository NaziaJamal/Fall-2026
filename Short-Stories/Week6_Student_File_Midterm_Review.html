Class AReview of Weeks 1–5

Points20 points (20% of your grade)
HomeworkNone this week
The Midterm: (1) a seminar discussion in a group for 15 minutes, graded on the quality of your ideas, evidence, and how you build on others; (2) an analytical essay of about 350 words on one story we studied, with a clear thesis and at least two direct quotations with section references (§).

Tips for success

  • Choose your best story and a backup. Know two short quotations from each.
  • Prepare three seminar contributions: a quotation you can explain, a connection between two stories, and an open question.
  • In the essay, write a thesis with HOW + WHAT + WHY, and analyze specific words.
  • Save 3 minutes at the end to check your writing.
