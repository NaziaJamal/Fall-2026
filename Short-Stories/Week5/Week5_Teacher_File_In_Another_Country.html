Content note

War injuries and disfigurement (§2–§4), fear of death (§5), and the death of the major’s young wife (§8). Read §8 aloud yourself and allow silence. The anthology’s biography mentions Hemingway’s suicide; the student materials do not. Tableaux are voluntary (students can “direct” instead). Full guidance is in the Lesson Plan tab, section 3.
