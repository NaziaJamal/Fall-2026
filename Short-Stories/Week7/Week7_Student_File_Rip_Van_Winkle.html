Homework

  • Analytical Essay 2 (300–350 words): Rip Van Winkle refuses what his village expects of him: hard work, obedience to his wife, and, after his sleep, a political side. How does Irving use the contrast between Rip’s life before and after his sleep to show the pressure to conform, and what does Rip gain or lose by escaping it? Use at least two direct quotations with section numbers (§). Start from your Worksheet Part 15 plan.
  • Reading Journal: Literary Lens concept (symbolism or irony), two Author’s Style examples (from Irving’s toolkit), a 3-sentence analytical response, and one discussion question.
  • Worksheet Part 2C: word forms.
Thesis frame: By contrasting Rip’s life before and after his sleep, Irving shows that ___ ; by escaping, Rip gains ___ but loses ___ .

Essay checklist

  • My thesis answers both parts of the question: how? and gain or lose?
  • I use at least two quotations with §.
  • I explain each quotation; I do not only retell the story.
  • I include a counter-argument (“Some readers think… However…”).
  • I use literary terms: symbol, irony, satire, frame narrator.
  • 300–350 words.
