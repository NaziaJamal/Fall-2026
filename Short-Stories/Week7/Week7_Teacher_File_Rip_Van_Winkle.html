Why this story: students asked for a lighter theme. “Rip Van Winkle” replaces the syllabus pairing for this week (“The Lottery” / “The Chrysanthemums”). The Lens keeps Symbolism, and Essay 2 keeps the syllabus question about conformity and its costs.
The text: the Reading tab has the whole story, adapted for B2–B2+. Nothing is cut: every event, character, and line of dialogue is there in the original order, plus Irving’s opening note, closing Note, and the optional Postscript.
Key structure: Class A reads the opening note and §1–§7 (jigsaw + your read-aloud) and ends with the Prediction Board (“Rip has fallen asleep. What happens next?”). Class B reads §8–§14, with the election scenes as Readers’ Theater. §8–§14 are locked in the Reading tab, and Class B is locked in students’ worksheets.

Content note

A light comic tale. Watch for dated gender stereotypes (the “henpecked husband” and the sharp-tongued wife), a wife’s death treated as a joke, drinking, and an angry political crowd (nobody is hurt). The original’s outdated words for Native peoples have been replaced with respectful terms. Full guidance is in the Lesson Plan tab, section 3.
