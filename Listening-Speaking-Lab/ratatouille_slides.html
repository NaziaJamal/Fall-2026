Warm-up  ·  Think · Pair · Share
What is your favorite food in the whole world?
Tell your partner: what is it, and who makes it best?
Warm-up  ·  Vote with your hands
Which one are you?
I love to cook
VS
I love to eat (not cook!)
Raise your hand for your side — then tell your partner why.
Warm-up  ·  Think · Pair · Share
Have you ever had a dream that other people thought was strange or impossible?
What was it? Did you follow it? Share with your partner.
Warm-up  ·  Predict!
The movie is called "Ratatouille."
It's the name of a French vegetable dish — but the hero is a rat! What do you think this story is about? Make a guess with your partner.
Now we watch
▶
Let's watch the first part of the movie!
About 20–25 minutes Watch for: Remy and his big dream Listen for: new food words
While you watch, notice: Who is Remy? What does he love? What problem does he have? We'll talk about it right after!
After watching  ·  Think · Pair · Share

Let's talk about what we saw!

Remember — give reasons: "I think… because…". There are no wrong answers.

How we'll do it: think for a moment, tell your partner, then we share around the class. Ready?
Discussion  ·  Think · Pair · Share
What is Remy's special talent?
Why is it unusual — even a problem — for a rat to have it?
Discussion  ·  Act it out!
Remy's family thinks his dream is crazy.
With your partner: one of you is Remy, one is his father. Remy, convince him your dream matters! (2 minutes)
Discussion  ·  Vote with your hands
Chef Gusteau's motto: "Anyone can cook."
I agree — anyone can!
VS
Not really — it takes talent
Vote, then defend your side. Does it only mean cooking, or anything in life?
Discussion  ·  Think · Pair · Share
Remy ends up alone in Paris, far from his family.
How do you think he feels? What would YOU do first, alone in a brand-new city?
Discussion  ·  Predict the rest!
What happens next?
With your group, predict: will Remy get to cook in a real kitchen? How? Each group shares one prediction.
Wrap-up  ·  Everyone answers
If you could be amazing at ONE thing — like Remy is at cooking — what would it be?
Go around your group. One sentence each: "I would be amazing at ______."
The end of today
Great talking, everyone!
