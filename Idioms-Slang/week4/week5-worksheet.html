The three word-order rules
1. Pronoun in the middle: pick her up · drop it off · figure it out · sort it out · give it up · stood me up
2. Never split: run into him · come up with it
3. No object: head out · come back · start over · grow apart

Part 6

Midterm Practice: Short Stories

Just like Part 1 of the midterm. Read each short story and choose the best answer. Only one answer is correct. Try to finish in 12 minutes.

  1. Mei just moved into a new building. Her neighbor stops by with cookies. They talk about the weather, the noisy elevator, and the best pizza place nearby. They are ______.
  2. Omar's flight was delayed four hours. He walked around the airport, bought a magazine, and called his mom. He was ______.
  3. Every morning, Sofia takes her son to daycare at 7:30 and leaves him there before work. She ______ at 7:30.
  4. Kenji was halfway to work when he realized his laptop was still on the kitchen table. He had to ______.
  5. At the hardware store, Lina saw her old English teacher. She didn't expect to see him there! She ______ him.
  6. Hassan tried to put together a new bookshelf for two hours. The instructions were confusing, but finally he ______.
  7. Rosa's coworker invited her to lunch on Friday. Rosa waited at the café for 40 minutes, but her coworker never came or called. Rosa ______.
  8. Two roommates argued about the rent. A week later, they sat down, said sorry, and made a plan. They ______.
  9. The bakery's first plan didn't work, so the owner thought of a new idea: weekend cooking classes. She ______ a new idea.
  10. Ali and his new coworker are very different, but they never argue and always help each other. In American English, Ali would say: “We ______ really well.”
