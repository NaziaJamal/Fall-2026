Bonus +50: the team uses the expression in a new, true sentence. Steal: if the team is wrong, another team can answer for the points.

How to play

  • Make 4 teams (or 2–6). Click a team name to rename it.
  • The team with the turn chooses a category and points. Bigger points = harder task.
  • The team has 30 seconds (45 for speaking tasks). One speaker answers, and the team can help.
  • Answer in a full sentence. Wrong or no answer? Another team can steal (15 seconds).
  • Bonus +50 for using the expression in a new, true sentence.
  • One secret tile is worth double points!
  • Final Challenge: every team bets points and tells a 60-second story with 3 expressions from different weeks.

Final Challenge: The Story Bet

Each team tells a 60-second story about a busy weekend. Use 3 expressions from 3 different weeks, with correct word order.

First, each team bets points (up to its score). The class and teacher judge: 3 correct, natural expressions = win the bet. Otherwise, lose it.
