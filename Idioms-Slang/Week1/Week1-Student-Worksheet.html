5. Put the conversation in order

👥 Pairs

Two neighbors meet at the mailbox. Number the lines 1–6. Then read the conversation aloud with your partner. Change roles!

Talk with your partner:
  1. Who broke the ice? How?
  2. What did they make small talk about?
  3. Do you think they will hit it off? Why?

C · Speak: Activation

≈ 55 min

7. Conversation Missions

👥 Pairs

Choose a card. Talk for 2–3 minutes. Use at least 2 expressions naturally. After each mission, change partners and choose a new card. In round 2, try not to look at the useful language!

1. OpenHi! Is this…?
2. Aska follow-up question
3. Sharesomething about you
4. CloseNice talking to you!

🙂 You can play yourself or invent a character.

8. Small talk topics: Safe or risky?

👨‍👩‍👧 Group

You are at a neighborhood party in the U.S. You just met someone. Can you talk about these topics? Agree as a group.

Group challenge: Write 3 great icebreaker questions for the party. Share them with the class. The class votes for the best one! 🏆

9. Story Chain: From Strangers to Friends

👨‍👩‍👧 Group

Write a short story together (6–8 sentences). Take turns: each person adds one sentence. Use the expressions in this order:

1 kill time2 break the ice3 make small talk4 chat5 hit it off

Start: “It was a rainy Monday, and the bus was 40 minutes late…”

🎤 Read your story to the class. Listeners: raise your hand when you hear an expression!
