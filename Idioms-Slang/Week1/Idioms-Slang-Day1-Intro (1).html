Course syllabus · class-by-class

Our 11 weeks: Weeks 1–6

WEEK 1

Small Talk & Ice-Breakers

Everyday conversation with neighbors, coworkers and strangers

break the ice · make small talk · shoot the breeze · kill time · hit it off

📝 “Shoot the breeze” is casual and a little old-fashioned. We'll also learn “chat”.

WEEK 2

Phrasal Verbs I: Movement & Direction

Meaning is not literal

pick up · drop off · turn around · run into · head out · come back

WEEK 3

Phrasal Verbs II: Change & Problem-Solving

Separable vs. inseparable

give up · start over · come up with · figure out · work out · sort out

📝 Grammar: with a pronoun, the verb must split: figure it out, not figure out it.

WEEK 4

Review & Consolidation

Weeks 1–3 expressions

Recycle Weeks 1–3

WEEK 5

Relationships & People

Friends, family and neighbors

grow apart · get along (with) · stand someone up · get on well (with) · patch things up

📝 In the U.S., “get along” is the everyday choice; “get on well” is more British. Grammar: stand someone up → got stood up.

WEEK 6 ⭐

MIDTERM

Reading in context + spoken exchange

Review of Weeks 1–5

📝 15 multiple-choice questions + a 2–3 minute dialogue using at least 3 expressions.
