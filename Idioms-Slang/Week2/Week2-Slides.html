Lina's Busy Saturday

Lina's sister texts: "Can you pick up Mom at the train station at 10?" Lina says, "Sure!"

First, Lina drops off her son at soccer practice. Then she heads out to the station. Ten minutes later… no phone! She turns around and drives back home to get it.

At the station, the train is late, so Lina kills time at the coffee cart. And guess who she runs into? Dev, her old coworker! They make small talk.

At 10:40, Mom arrives. Lina texts the coach: "So sorry, running late! I'll come back for Omar at 12:15."

Grammar · Phrasal verb patterns

Three kinds of phrasal verbs

① Separable

pick up · drop off

✔ pick her up
pick up her

② Inseparable

run into

✔ ran into her
ran her into

③ No object

head out · come back · turn around

✔ We headed out at 7.
✔ come back home

⚠ "ran him into…" sounds like a car crash: "He ran his bike into a van." 🚲💥

Would you say this here? 👍 🤔 👎

📱 Text to your best friend: "omw! I'll pick u up in 5"👍
👩‍💼 To your manager: "I'm going to head out. See you tomorrow!"👍
👩‍💼 To your manager: "Aight, I'm gonna bounce."👎
🚏 To a stranger: "I will collect my daughter from school."🤔
💼 Job interview: "I can pick up extra shifts on weekends."👍
👀 Slang to recognize: bounce · dip = leave (very casual). Understand them, but for you, head out is the safe choice.
