Personal topics are always optional. On any card, students can play themselves or invent a character. Cards marked Week 1 recycle last week's expressions.

A · Role-Play Cards pairs · 3–4 min each · rotate partners

B · Conversation Missions: Round 1 with support

2–3 minutes. Use at least 2 expressions naturally. Don't read. Just talk!

C · Conversation Missions: Round 2 new partner · less support · a twist!

New partner, new card. No sentence starters this time. Halfway through, Partner B reads the twist aloud.

D · The Excuse Generator teams · 2 min to plan · 1 min to tell

Your team is late. Invent the most creative but believable excuse. Use 3+ phrasal verbs (any week). The class votes for the best one.
