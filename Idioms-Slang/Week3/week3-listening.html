Part 2

Dialogue: "No Heat in October"

Rosa & Kevin, coworkers · about 1 minute.

Before you listen (class)

It's October in Massachusetts. Your apartment heat stops working. What would you do first? Who would you call?

The audio uses your device's voice. If you hear nothing, check your volume, or try Chrome or Safari.

Rosa:Morning! You look tired.
Kevin:Ugh, I am. The heat in my apartment isn't working, and it was like forty degrees last night.
Rosa:In October? Did you call your landlord?
Kevin:Three times. No answer. I almost gave up and bought a space heater.
Rosa:Okay, don't give up yet. Did you check the thermostat? Sometimes it's just the batteries.
Kevin:I tried. I couldn't figure it out. The screen just says "Error 4."
Rosa:Hmm. My building had the same problem last year. We called the management company directly, not the landlord. They sorted it out in one day.
Kevin:Really? I didn't know that was an option.
Rosa:Yeah. And write everything down: dates, times, who you talked to.
Kevin:That's smart. I need to come up with a plan, because I'm not sleeping in my coat again.
Rosa:Ha! You'll work it out. Want to grab a coffee before the meeting?
Kevin:Definitely. Then I'll call them on my lunch break.

Listen 1 · True or False?

  2. Kevin bought a space heater.

What does it mean here?

Kevin says, "I almost gave up and bought a space heater." Did he buy one?

🏠 Good to know (Massachusetts): The heating season is September 15 to June 15. Homes must be at least 68°F during the day (7 a.m.–11 p.m.) and 64°F at night. In Boston, you can call 311 or Inspectional Services if the heat isn't working. Source: City of Boston, Inspectional Services.
