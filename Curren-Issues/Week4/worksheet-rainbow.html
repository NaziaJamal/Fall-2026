Human Rainbow

Move
Take a color card. Don't show it! Walk around and ask: “What color do you have?” Find the other person with your color. Then the class makes a rainbow: stand in the correct order from red to violet.
What color do you have? · I have ___. · I think red is first. · Orange is next to red. · Green is between yellow and blue.

Now write the 7 colors in order.

Talk: What does a rainbow mean in your country or culture?

Where Should You Stand?

Move
Your teacher puts a SUN sign on one wall and a RAIN sign on the other wall. Stand where you can see a rainbow! Then your teacher moves the signs. Move again! Explain your place to a partner.
The sun is behind me. · The rain is in front of me. · I am between the sun and the rain.

Look at the picture. Choose the correct words.

5

Be the Light! Act It Out

Move
Groups of 4–5. One student is the raindrop (make a big circle with your arms). The others are sunlight. Act out the steps below: walk into the raindrop, bend, turn back, and leave. One student reads each step aloud. Then put the steps in order.

Cause and result: match the two halves

Classroom Color Hunt

Move
Pairs. You have 5 minutes. Walk around the classroom. Find something for each rainbow color. Write what it is and where it is. Use a different preposition each time! Then read 3 sentences to another pair — they must find the things.
There is a red bag under the table. · There are blue pens next to the computer.

10

Write: My Rainbow Memory

Writing

Think of a time you saw a rainbow (or a beautiful sky). Write 5–6 sentences. Say when, where, who was with you, and what you saw.

Last year, in June, I saw a big rainbow in Medellín, Colombia. It was in the late afternoon, at about 5:00. I was at the park with my sister. The sun was behind us, and the rain was in front of us. We could see all seven colors! We took photos, but the rainbow moved when we walked.
When? in + month/year · on + day/date · at + time  ·  Where? in + city/country · at + the park/home/school  ·  behind · in front of · above

Answer Key

Teacher

1. Rainbow order: red · orange · yellow · green · blue · indigo · violet

2. Words: 1 sunlight · 2 raindrop · 3 appear · 4 bend · 5 spread apart · 6 at the same time · 7 low in the sky · 8 in front of

3. Main idea: b

4. Picture: 1 behind · 2 in front of · 3 between · 4 in front of · 5 above

5. Steps: 1 Rain and sunlight happen at the same time. · 2 Sunlight goes into a raindrop and bends. · 3 Each color bends a little differently, so the colors spread apart. · 4 Some light turns back inside the raindrop. · 5 The light bends again when it leaves the raindrop. · 6 Colored light from many raindrops reaches your eyes.

Cause and result: 1 c · 2 a · 3 d · 4 b

6. True / False: 1 F (it looks white, but it has many colors) · 2 T · 3 F (each color bends a little differently) · 4 F (easier when the sun is low: morning or late afternoon) · 5 F (you can't walk up to a rainbow) · 6 T

7. Where is it? 1 above · 2 behind · 3 in front of · 4 under · 5 between · 6 next to

8. Chunks: at the same time = together · in front of you = on the side you are looking at · turns back = goes back the other way · low in the sky = not high, near the ground

in · on · at: 1 in · 2 in · 3 at · 4 on · 5 in · 6 at
