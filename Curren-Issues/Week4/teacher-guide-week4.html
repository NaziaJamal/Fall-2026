Week 4 Teacher Guide · The Pumpkin and the Rainbow

Current Issues · Basic Level (A1–A2+) · Two 100-minute classes + 30-minute break · ESA

Overview

Syllabus focusNumbers, dates, and places in the news
Language focusNumbers, prices, dates · prepositions of time and place: in / on / at · along, past, down · behind, in front of, between, above
SLO (Week 4)Students understand and say numbers, prices, dates, and places from an article correctly.
TextsClass 1: “A Giant Pumpkin on the Move” (news, A2, Oct 20, 2026, American audio) · Class 2: “How Does a Rainbow Form?” (explainer, A2, American audio)

Class 1 story in short

On a Sunday, strong winds blew a giant inflatable jack-o'-lantern — as tall as a two-story building — away from Phillips Farm in Cary, North Carolina. It rolled along a fence, past a church, and down the road. Workers stopped it in about one minute. Now ropes, metal spikes, and two tractors keep it in place.

Class 2 text in short

A rainbow can appear when there is rain and sunlight at the same time. The sun must be behind you and the rain in front of you. Light bends when it enters a raindrop, the colors spread apart, some light turns back, and it bends again as it leaves. Rainbows are easier to see when the sun is low.

How the two classes connect

  • Class 1 = places and dates in the news: a route (along, past, down), a fact file of numbers and places, in / on / at, and prices at a farm stand.
  • Class 2 = where and when: where to stand (behind, in front of, between), place prepositions, time chunks, and a personal “when and where” story.
  • Both worksheets have 4–5 movement activities (orange in the worksheets) so energy stays high across 200 minutes.

Classroom

Safety for movement activities: walking only — no running, even in “Run to the Wall.” In Pumpkin Escape, the “pumpkin” keeps eyes open. Offer a seated role (checker, reader, timekeeper) for anyone who prefers not to move.

Quality Check

  • Follows the syllabus: Week 4 numbers, dates, places; prepositions of time and place
  • SLO practiced in both classes: fact file, prices, route, where/when writing
  • ESA in each class: Engage (move, predict) → Study (words, reading, language) → Activate (role-play, hunt, writing)
  • Both classes exactly 100 minutes; 30-minute break included
  • 9 movement activities across the day, with seated roles available
  • Interaction: individual, pairs, groups, whole class
  • Productive outcomes: role-play receipts, color-hunt sentences, news story + rainbow memory
  • Class 2 builds on Class 1 (places → where/when; in/on/at place → time)
  • A1 support (pictures, dropdowns, frames) and A2+ challenge (corrections, extra facts, leading roles)
  • Adult-appropriate: real news, real science, real shopping
