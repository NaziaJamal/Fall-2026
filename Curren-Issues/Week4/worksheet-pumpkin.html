Human Story Line

Move
Groups of 6: each person gets one event card from your teacher. Read your card aloud. Stand in a line in the correct order — from first to last. Then each person says their sentence with First, Then, Next, After that, Finally.

Writing

Something else is “on the move”! Choose an idea (or your own) and write a short news story: 5–6 sentences. Use a place, a day or date, a number, and along / past / down.

HEADLINEGiant Balloon Flies Over a School
On Saturday, a giant yellow balloon flew away from a party in Lowell, Massachusetts. It was as big as a car! The wind carried it past a school and along the river. Two police officers followed it for 20 minutes. Finally, it stopped in a tree. Nobody was hurt.
