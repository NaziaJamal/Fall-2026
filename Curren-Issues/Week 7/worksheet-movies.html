Two Big Movies on the Same Day

Two very big movies come out on the same day: December 18, 2026. One is Avengers: Doomsday, a superhero movie from Marvel. The other is Dune: Part Three, a science fiction movie. Fans call the weekend “Dunesday.”

Movie theaters are happy, but they are also worried. Many of the same people want to see both movies. Theaters have only a few very big screens, like IMAX screens. The two movies must share them.

In 2023, two very different movies, Barbie and Oppenheimer, came out on the same day. People called it “Barbenheimer,” and both movies did very well.

What will happen this time? Experts think Avengers will sell more tickets than Dune. But together, the two movies may make the biggest movie weekend ever!

Dunesday: Two Giants, One Weekend

Two of the most popular movies of the year, Marvel's Avengers: Doomsday and Warner Bros.' Dune: Part Three, will open on the same day: December 18, 2026. Fans have a name for the big weekend: “Dunesday.”

Earlier this year, many theater owners were nervous. The two movies have a similar audience, so they may compete for the same people. They also need the same special screens, like IMAX, and there are not many of them. One theater worker said that one of the movies had to move. But neither studio changed its date.

It is different from “Barbenheimer” in 2023. Barbie and Oppenheimer were very different movies, so they attracted different people, and both were big hits.

Now, in November, the numbers look exciting. One expert, Shawn Robbins of Box Office Theory, thinks Avengers: Doomsday could make $340 million or more in its first weekend, and Dune: Part Three about $100 million. Together with other movies, theaters could earn more than $440 million — the biggest weekend in movie history.

Help words: studio = a company that makes movies · similar = almost the same · attract = make people want to come · a hit = a very popular movie or song · expert = a person who knows a lot about something · earn = get money for work or sales
