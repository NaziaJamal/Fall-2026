The Little Black Bird That Won

Every year, New Zealand has a competition: Bird of the Year. People vote for their favorite bird. In 2026, the winner was the black robin.

The black robin is a small black bird. It lives only on the Chatham Islands. These islands are far from the rest of New Zealand — about 800 kilometers (500 miles).

Many years ago, the bird had big problems. Its homes disappeared, and people brought new animals to the islands. These pests hurt the birds. In 1980, there were only five black robins in the world!

Then people helped. They put black robin eggs in the nests of other birds. The other birds took care of the baby robins. Now there are about 350 black robins. That is many more than in 1980. But the bird is still in danger. It needs a new, safe island.

A Small Bird with a Big Comeback

The black robin, once called the rarest bird in the world, won New Zealand's 2026 Bird of the Year competition. The prize celebrates its amazing recovery: from just five birds to about 350 adults.

This small black bird lives only on the Chatham Islands, about 800 kilometers (500 miles) east of mainland New Zealand. Habitat loss and pests that people brought to the islands almost destroyed the population. By 1980, only five birds were left, and only one pair could have babies: a female called Old Blue and a male called Old Yellow.

Conservationists tried something unusual. They put black robin eggs into the nests of other kinds of birds, and those birds raised the robin chicks as their own. The plan worked, and slowly the population grew.

With its black feathers, black eyes, and small black beak, the robin is sometimes called the “goth” of New Zealand birds. The competition received a record 105,000 votes. But the black robin is still threatened. It lives on only two protected islands with no pests, and there is almost no space left there. To keep growing, it needs a third pest-free island.

Help words: rarest = there are fewer than any other · habitat loss = animals lose the places where they live · conservationist = a person who protects nature · raise = take care of a baby until it grows · goth = a style with black clothes and dark makeup · protected = kept safe by law
