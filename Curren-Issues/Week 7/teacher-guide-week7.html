Overview

Syllabus focusTwo stories, two views — comparing. Theme: work and jobs.
Language focusComparatives (smaller, rarer, more famous, better) · but · both… and…
SLO (Week 7)Students compare two short stories or two opinions using simple comparison language.
TextsClass 1: “Black Robin Wins New Zealand's Bird of the Year” (Level 3, Nov 15, 2026) · Class 2: “Two Big Movies, One Weekend” (Level 3)

Class 1 story in short

The black robin won New Zealand's 2026 Bird of the Year with a record 105,000 votes. It lives only on the Chatham Islands, about 800 km (500 miles) from mainland New Zealand. Habitat loss and pests left only five birds in 1980. Conservationists put robin eggs in other birds' nests, and those birds raised the chicks. Today there are about 350 adults — but the bird is still threatened and needs a third pest-free island.

How the week works (Option B: two stories)

  • Class 1 compares inside one story: then vs. now (1980 vs. 2026) and bird vs. bird. Students learn comparatives and but.
  • Class 2 compares two stories: the bird competition and the movie competition. Students add both… and… and give an opinion about the two stories.
  • Both articles are Level 3 (B1), so each worksheet has an easier retelling (Story A) and a challenge version (Story B), with a link to the original.
