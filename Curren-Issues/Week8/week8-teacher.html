Celebrations Around the World

Students read how Americans celebrated Thanksgiving 2025. Then they become the news: each student retells a celebration from their own country in 4–6 sentences, in order, using the past simple.

Before Class

  • Add 4 photos to Slides → Mystery Celebrations (slide 5): lanterns, Lunar New Year decorations, a Day of the Dead altar, a parade balloon. No famous people or brand characters.
  • Print and cut from Lesson Plan → Print cards: Pass the Plate cards, story strips, prediction cards, role cards, and the FACT / FICTION / AGREE / DISAGREE signs.
  • Bring paper plates, a soft ball, sticky notes, and a music playlist.
  • Post the Student file link on Community.

Sensitivity: a student's “celebration” can be any happy event (a birthday, a wedding, a festival). If students ask about the origin of Thanksgiving, keep it short and neutral, then return to how people celebrate today. Details are in the Lesson Plan.
