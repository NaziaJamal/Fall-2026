Celebrations Around the World

This week we read how Americans celebrated Thanksgiving. Then YOU tell the story of a celebration from your country.

This Week I Can…

  • find the who, what, where, when and why in a news story.
  • use 8 new words about celebrations.
  • use the past: traveled, went, ate, got together…
  • tell a story in order with First, Then, After that, Finally.
  • ask and answer questions about a celebration: What did you do?
