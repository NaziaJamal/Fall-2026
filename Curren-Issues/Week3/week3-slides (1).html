First Reading: The Main Idea

Study

Read quickly (4 minutes). What is the story mainly about?

A
The Bank of England will put pictures of animals on its new banknotes. ✓
B
Almost 500,000 people bought new banknotes last week.
C
Winston Churchill designed the new banknotes.
Tip: the headline and the first sentence usually tell you the main idea.
10 min: silent reading 4 min → answer alone → compare (2 min) → check (2 min) → ask why B and C are wrong (2 min): B — people voted, they didn't buy notes; C — Churchill is a famous person who will leave the notes. Worksheet Section 4.

Second Reading: The 5 Ws

Study

Read again carefully. Write short answers (Worksheet Section 5).

Who?Who is making the new notes? → the Bank of England
What?What will be on the notes? → a puffin, a barn owl, a bumblebee, a hedgehog
Where?Which country is it? → the UK (Britain)
When?When will the notes be ready? → not yet — it could take years
Why?Why these animals? → people voted for them
15 min: individual 6 min → pairs compare 4 min → check together 5 min. A2+ extras: almost 500,000 people voted; the puffin was chosen for sea animals; the king stays on the other side.

Word Families

Study

One word can have many forms. The ending tells you the job of the word.

A person
-er / -or

help → helper
vote → voter
design → designer

Describes
-ful

help → helpful
use → useful
color → colorful

A thing
-ment / -tion

replace → replacement
collect → collection
pay → payment

Almost 500,000 voters chose the animals. · A designer will draw the notes. · The notes must be useful.
Word families block = 20 min: this slide 5 → race 10 → worksheet 5. Elicit: “A person who votes is a…?” “A person who teaches?” Some words are the same as a verb and a noun: vote (v) / a vote (n), design (v) / a design (n).
