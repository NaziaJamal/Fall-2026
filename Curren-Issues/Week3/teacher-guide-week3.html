Article“A New Look for Banknotes” · ESL News Stories · Level 2 (A2) · Oct 11, 2026 · Audio: British narrator

The story in short

The Bank of England will put four animals — a puffin, a barn owl, a bumblebee, and a hedgehog — on its new £5, £10, £20, and £50 notes. They will replace famous people like Winston Churchill. Almost 500,000 people voted for their favorite animals. The king stays on the other side. The new notes are not ready; designing, testing, and printing could take years. They must be durable, easy to use, and difficult to counterfeit.

Why this article works

  • Money is universal — every student can talk about the money in their country (11 countries in class).
  • Strong word families appear naturally: vote → voter, design → designer, replace → replacement.
  • Numbers (£5–£50, 500,000) preview Week 4 (numbers and prices).
  • Watch for: British English — note / banknote = US bill; pounds (£). Point it out early.

Quality Check

  • Follows the syllabus: Week 3 vocabulary + word families
  • SLO addressed directly: My 6 Words + writing
  • ESA: Engage (money, animals) → Study (words, reading, word families) → Activate (speaking, design, writing)
  • Both classes exactly 100 minutes; 30-minute break included
  • Interaction: individual, pairs, teams, groups, whole class
  • Movement: Guess the Money teams, Line-Up, Charades, T/F race
  • Productive outcome: a group banknote design + a 5–7 sentence paragraph
  • Class 2 builds on Class 1 (new tasks, no re-reading)
  • A1 support (frames, choices, hint lines) and A2+ challenge (experts, presenter role, extra facts)
  • Adult-appropriate: real money, real countries, real decisions
