Word Families

Study Notes · Current Issues · Basic Level · Week 3 · “A New Look for Banknotes”

1. What is a word family?

A word family is a group of words from the same root. The words are related, but each one has a different job in the sentence.

ROOT
help
Verb · action
help

Can you help me?

Person
helper

She is a good helper.

Adjective · describes
helpful

My teacher is very helpful.

Learn one word → you can understand three or four words!

3. Endings tell you the job

-er / -or
A person

vote → voter

design → designer

teach → teacher

act → actor

collect → collector

-ment / -tion
A thing (noun)

replace → replacement

pay → payment

collect → collection

inform → information

-ful
Describes (adjective)

help → helpful

use → useful

color → colorful

care → careful

Careful: -er can also be a machine: a printer, a computer, a dishwasher. And -ful has only one L: helpful, not helpfull.

5. Which form do I need? Look at the words around the gap

After a / an / the / my → noun

The designer drew a puffin. · I need a replacement.

After will / can / to / please → verb

Animals will replace people. · I want to vote.

After is / very / so → adjective

The map is very useful. · She is so helpful.

6. Common mistakes

He is a good design.
He is a good designer.
A person needs -er.
This app is very use.
This app is very useful.
After very, use an adjective (-ful).
My friend is very helpfull.
My friend is very helpful.
-ful has one L.
Many voter chose the owl.
Many voters chose the owl.
More than one person → add -s: voters, designers.
Remember:  -er / -or = a person  ·  -ment / -tion = a thing  ·  -ful = describes  ·  Look at the word before the gap!
