✏️ There is / There are

Study

From the story:

There is + one

There is a famous study about jam.
There is one small table.

There are + two or more

There are two tables.
There are 24 kinds of jam.

Questions

Is there a park near your home?
Are there many choices?

Short answers

Yes, there is. / No, there isn't.
Yes, there are. / No, there aren't.
Elicit before showing: “How many tables are there in the jam study?” → “There are two tables.” Concept check: “Is ‘there are’ for one thing or many?” Drill the questions with rising intonation. (Grammar block = 22 min: this slide + next ≈ 5 min, practice ≈ 17 min.)

🛒 The Smart Shopper Challenge

Groups

The situation

A new student arrives in our city this week. She goes to a big supermarket. There are 40 kinds of bread and 30 kinds of rice! She feels stressed. Help her!

Your group (3–4)

Write 3 tips for her.
Give a reason for each tip.
Choose your #1 tip.

Roles

✍️ Writer · ⏱️ Timekeeper
🗣️ Presenter · 💡 Idea leader
Tip 1: Make a short list because . · Our #1 tip is ___ because ___.
20 min total: prepare 12 min (this slide) → present 8 min (next slide). Mixed-level groups; give A2+ students the Presenter or Idea leader role. Groups write tips on paper or the board. Ideas from the article: a short list, “good enough” is OK, ask a friend; plus their own: go to a small store, buy the same brand, ask the staff.
