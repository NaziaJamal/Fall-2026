Smelling Chocolate Could Help You Exercise

Could the smell of chocolate help you exercise longer? A new study suggests that it might.

Researchers asked a group of young men to smell dark chocolate, milk chocolate, or water before and during a leg exercise. All of the men lifted weights regularly. During the experiment, they smelled the chocolate but did not eat it.

After smelling chocolate, the men were able to do more reps. Dark chocolate had the strongest effect, while milk chocolate also improved their performance.

The two scents seemed to work differently. Dark chocolate reduced the men’s appetite, so they felt less hungry. However, they found the sweeter smell of milk chocolate more pleasant. Researchers are not yet sure why either scent helped them exercise longer.

The results are surprising. The study was small and only included young men who exercised regularly. Researchers now want to repeat the experiment with different groups to learn more about how smells affect exercise.
