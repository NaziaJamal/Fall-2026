1. The big idea

These three small words join two sentences into one longer sentence.

I was hungry.+I ate a sandwich.
I was hungry,soI ate a sandwich.

2. Three words, three jobs

because
Reason
It answers: Why?
what happened + because + why

I'm tired because I worked late.

She is happy because it's Friday.

so
Result
It answers: What happened next?
why , + so + what happened

I worked late, so I'm tired.

It was raining, so we stayed home.

but
Contrast
It shows: a different idea
idea 1 , + but + a different idea

I like coffee, but I don't like tea.

The test was hard, but I passed.

3. because and so: the same idea, opposite order

REASONIt was cold
so
RESULTI wore a jacket
RESULTI wore a jacket
because
REASONit was cold

so goes after the reason. because goes before the reason.

4. One story, three ways

SO · RESULTI was hungry, so I ate a big lunch.
BECAUSE · REASONI ate a big lunch because I was hungry.
BUT · CONTRASTI was hungry, but I didn't eat. I was too busy!

5. In our news story

becauseThe men felt less hungry because they smelled dark chocolate.
soDark chocolate reduced their appetite, so they felt less hungry.
butThey smelled the chocolate, but they did not eat it.
butDark and milk chocolate are both nice, but dark chocolate seems stronger.

6. Commas

so and but: use a comma

It was late, so I went home.
I like it, but it's expensive.

because: usually no comma

I went home because it was late.
Starting with Because? Then use a comma in the middle: Because it was late, I went home.

7. Common mistakes

Because I was hungry, so I ate.
Because I was hungry, I ate.
Use because OR so, not both in one sentence.
I was tired because I went to bed early.
I was tired, so I went to bed early.
Going to bed early is the result, not the reason. Use so.
Because I like chocolate.
I eat chocolate every day because I like it.
In writing, a sentence with because needs two parts. (In speaking, “Because I like it!” is OK as a short answer.)
I like the gym but, I don't go.
I like the gym, but I don't go.
The comma goes before but and so.
Remember:  because → Why?  ·  so → What happened?  ·  but → Something different!
