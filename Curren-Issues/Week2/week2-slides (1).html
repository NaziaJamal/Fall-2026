First Reading: The Main Idea

Study

Read quickly (4 minutes). What is the story mainly about?

A
Smelling chocolate may help people exercise longer. ✓
B
Dark chocolate may make people feel less hungry during exercise.
C
Young men found milk chocolate more pleasant than dark chocolate.
B and C are true, but they are details. The main idea is the big idea of the whole story.
10 min: silent reading 4 min → answer alone → compare with a partner (2 min) → check (2 min) → teach main idea vs. detail (2 min). Go back to Think Ahead: was your prediction right?

Second Reading: The 5 Ws

Study

Read again carefully. Write short answers. Then compare with a partner.

Who?Who was in the study? → researchers and young men who lift weights
What?What did the men do? → smelled chocolate or water and did a leg exercise
When?When did they smell it? → before and during the exercise
Where?Where was the study? → The story doesn't say!
Why?Why did it help? → Researchers are not sure yet.
15 min: individual 6 min → pairs compare 4 min → check together 5 min. Teaching point: not every news story answers all 5 Ws — “Where?” is missing and “Why?” is unknown. Ask A2+: “What else do we know about Why?” (dark chocolate = less hungry; milk chocolate = more pleasant).

True or False Race

Teams

Teams of 3. Stand up for TRUE. Stay seated for FALSE. If it's false, correct it!

1The men ate chocolate before the leg exercise. FALSE → they only smelled it
2Milk chocolate had a stronger effect than dark chocolate. FALSE → dark was stronger
3The men found the smell of milk chocolate more pleasant. TRUE
4All of the men lifted weights regularly. TRUE
5The study was very big. FALSE → it was small
6 min. 1 point for the right answer, 1 bonus point for a correct sentence for FALSE items. Items 1–3 are from the worksheet; 4–5 are extra.

because · so · but

Study
Reason
because

The men felt less hungry because they smelled dark chocolate.

Result
so

Dark chocolate reduced their appetite, so they felt less hungry.

Contrast
but

They smelled the chocolate, but they didn't eat it.

because → why?  ·  so → what happened?  ·  but → something different
Language block = 15 min: this slide 5 min (elicit: “Why did they feel less hungry?”), practice 10 min. Note the comma before so and but.
