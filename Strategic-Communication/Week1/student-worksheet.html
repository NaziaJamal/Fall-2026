Giving a first impression

  • My first reaction is that…
  • At first glance, this seems…
  • I'd rank this higher / lower because…
  • I'm not sure yet. I'd need to know who / when / why…

Analyzing

  • The main goal here seems to be…
  • This is aimed at… rather than…
  • The tone feels… which suggests…
  • The word "…" is doing a lot of work here.
  • What's missing from this message is…

Agreeing and disagreeing politely

  • That's a fair point, and I'd add that…
  • I see it differently. To me…
  • I'm not entirely convinced. What about…?
  • I partly agree, but…

Explaining why something worked or failed

  • I think this worked because…
  • This failed because the communicator didn't consider…
  • It was effective, but was it honest?
  • It reached the right people / the wrong people.

Changing your mind

  • Now that I know…, I'd change my rating to…
  • That changes things. Before, I thought… but now…
  • I'd keep my rating, because…
