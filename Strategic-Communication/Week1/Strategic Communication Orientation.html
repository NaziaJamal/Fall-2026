Our analytical spine · used in every class

The GAME framework

G

Goal

What does the communicator actually want to happen?

"What's the real objective here?"
A

Audience

Who is the message really for, and what do they need to hear?

"Who is really listening?"
M

Message

What is the core idea, and what is left out?

"What are they really saying?"
E

Expression

Tone and channel: how and where it is said.

"Does the delivery fit?"

You will receive a GAME reference card today. Keep it for the whole term.

The 4A apology framework

1

Acknowledge

Name clearly what happened.

2

Accept

Take responsibility without shifting the blame.

3

Apologize

Express real regret to the people affected.

4

Act

Commit to concrete steps that fix the problem.

Every apology gets a verdict:SuccessFailureMixed

Assessment policies

This course assesses thinking out loud

→

Spoken, live, in class

No written homework. Everything is assessed in the room.

→

Groups, but you are seen

Group work is the default. Your own contribution is assessed through delivery and Q&A.

→

Midterm and final

The midterm covers Classes 1–6. The War Room uses every framework from the term.

→

No scripts

The War Room topic is revealed on the day, to keep it authentic.

→

Participation counts

Consistent, good-faith contribution to discussion, activities and peer questions.

→

Make-ups

Only with prior notice and documented justification, such as medical or emergency.
